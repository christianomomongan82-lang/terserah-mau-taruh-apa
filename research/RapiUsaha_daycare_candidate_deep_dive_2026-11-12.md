### Daycare software and product gap

**What free software already does**
- NestliCare says daycare software is $0 with every feature included and no paid tier; it lists attendance/check-in, daily reports/photos, meal tracking/menus, health checks/incident reports, billing/invoicing/online payments, staff management, parent messaging, authorized pickup, family profiles, enrollment forms/e-signatures, child files and parent/staff apps.
- This means we should NOT build an app, spreadsheet or admin dashboard that duplicates daily attendance, billing, parent communication or child tracking. A free competitor already offers an integrated product.
- Source: https://nestlicare.com/pricing and https://nestlicare.com/free-daycare-software

**What remains plausible**
- The paid Etsy starter kit is positioned as documents for providers starting and setting up their daycare, not as monthly-use software. It includes customized/printable material; its item-level reviews prove some buyers pay for that convenience even though digital software exists.
- The listing's 275 pages may be excessive for a small provider, and Canva editing is not ideal on a phone. A smaller, well-structured, phone-friendly document pack could be a useful alternative, but that differentiation is still a hypothesis—not buyer-validated.
- Candidate scope to validate:
  1. New-family intake / child profile questionnaire (non-medical, non-legal).
  2. First-week onboarding checklist and parent welcome packet.
  3. Daily parent communication report (meals, nap, activity, notes; no medical diagnosis).
  4. Attendance / sign-in tracking and supply/meal planner as printable admin aids.
  5. Editable parent message templates and simple operating checklists.
- Explicitly exclude from first version: legally binding childcare contracts, state licensing claims, regulatory compliance handbooks, emergency response instructions, medication authorization forms, medical advice, and documents implying legal protection. Those require competent local/legal review and differ by jurisdiction.
- Position the product as optional admin/communication paperwork, not a replacement for licensed software, official forms, legal documents, or local requirements.

## Distribution / eligibility gate

- Etsy has the strongest discovery channel evidence for digital downloads, but account owners must be 18; 13–17 can only sell under a parent/guardian-owned account with direct supervision. User does not want guardian involvement, so no Etsy account should be opened before the user's own age eligibility.
- Current interim path could only be a platform that genuinely accepts a 17-year-old seller and provides compliant payouts. Kreavo Terms appear to accept from age 17, but its public buyer base remains unproven and it must be tested for bank account ownership/KYC, payment methods for target-country buyers and digital delivery. Do not assume it offers international buyer reach.
- User's BRI/DANA availability is user-stated, but ownership, KYC status and commercial payout eligibility are unknown. Before any launch, verify these facts and the platform's official current terms.
- User's target first income ideally <30 days conflicts with Etsy age gate because turning 18 is Dec 25, 2026, more than 30 days after Nov 12, 2026. The only responsible interim option must pass the age/KYC/payout hard gate; do not launch through false age or another person's identity.

## Recommendation (ASSISTANT_PROPOSAL, NOT USER_APPROVED)

1. Stop expanding RapiPO and do not build the mobile detailing/embroidery/candle generic templates.
2. Validate a **Home Daycare Launch & Parent Onboarding Pack** as the next product hypothesis. Focus on paperwork convenience and first-family onboarding—not software functions already free, and not state-specific legal forms.
3. Investigate the 93-review competitor's buyer expectations, page structure, editable/printable formats, and review complaints; then compare with other daycare document listings before designing.
4. Validate whether buyers value a smaller, clearer, phone-friendly pack more than a huge 275-page kit. Do not assume this gap exists solely because competitor's Canva mobile is glitchy.
5. Hard-gate: verify legal seller/payout route, buyer-country checkout support and file delivery. No product launch until this works.
6. Keep final product, exact package, price and marketplace OPEN until the user approves.
