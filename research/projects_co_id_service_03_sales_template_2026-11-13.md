## Service concept
**Title:** Jasa Pembuatan Template Rekap Penjualan Excel Sederhana

A reusable blank workbook for one sales-recording workflow. The buyer receives a structured input sheet and an automatic 12-month summary; this is distinct from Service 01 data entry and Service 02 data cleaning.

### Proposed fixed scope
- One Excel workbook (.xlsx).
- Three sheets: Panduan, Input Penjualan, Ringkasan.
- Input structure: date, transaction number, product name, quantity, unit price, discount, and automatic total.
- Preconfigured capacity: up to 500 transaction rows.
- Automatic yearly summary for 12 months: sales total, transaction count, and total units sold.
- One monthly sales chart.
- Year selector on the summary sheet.
- One minor revision within the original scope.
- No call or video meeting required; requirements are confirmed by platform chat.

### Exclusions
No inventory tracking, purchasing, expense/profit/loss, COGS, taxes, accounts receivable/payable, multi-branch reporting, marketplace API integration, scraping, macro/VBA, complex formulas, or importing/cleaning historical transaction datasets. This template reports sales after the entered discount; it does not calculate net profit.
